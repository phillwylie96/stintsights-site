What your team gets

Everything from the garage to the pit wall

Drivers run a small Windows app while they race. Every lap lands in your team's space, and your engineer can watch the race live from any browser.

Lap analysis

  • Compare any two laps from anyone on the team, with sector times and the gap at every corner.
  • Synced traces for speed, throttle, brake, steering, gear and RPM, with zoom and replay.
  • Track map and racing lines built from your own laps, on any circuit.
  • Theoretical best and pace bands against your reference times.

Live pit wall

  • Fuel and virtual energy: laps left, which runs out first, pit window, how much to add.
  • "If you pit this lap": where you'd rejoin, learned from every car that pits at that track.
  • Battles, traffic and rivals' stops, with a timing tower filtered by class.
  • Tyres, brakes and damage, plus radio-style alerts. Works on a phone.

Discord built in

  • Race results posted automatically to your results channel, under your team's name and logo.
  • Driver keys by slash command, so adding a new driver takes seconds.
  • A ready-made setup channel with the app download and how-to guide.

Your team's brand

  • Your logo, colours and headline font on every page and in the PC app.
  • Your own address, like yourteam.stintsights.racing.
  • Your data stays yours: only your drivers and engineers see your laps.
The live pit wall

Know when to box before your driver asks

The pit wall reads the race once a second. It works out fuel and virtual energy together, spots who's catching whom, and tells you where you'd come out if you pitted this lap. It counts the cars that are pitting too.

Pit lane times are learned from real stops at each track, so predictions get sharper the more you race.

Questions

Good to know

Which sims does it support?

Le Mans Ultimate. It's built around LMU's fuel, virtual energy and multi-class racing, from Hypercar to GT3.

Will it slow my game down?

No. The app only reads the data LMU already shares while you drive, and it's very light. It never changes anything in the game.

What does each driver need?

A Windows PC running LMU, the StintSights app and a personal key, which they get from a command in your Discord.

Who can see our laps?

Only your team. Every team's data is kept separate, and you can ask for a driver's or the whole team's data to be deleted at any time.

How much does it cost?

We're starting with a small pilot. Pilot teams get early pricing and help shape what comes next. Register your interest below.

Can our engineer use a phone?

Yes. The pit wall works on a phone or tablet, so your engineer can follow the race from anywhere.
